Key Findings
📈 Peak month — October 2024: £192,662 revenue driven by Electronics demand in North region. Top performing region: North at £449,767.
⚠️ West region volatility: 94% revenue swing between April and June 2024 — signals instability, not growth. Requires operations investigation before interpretation as a trend.
